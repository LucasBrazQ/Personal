## August Performance Overview

August was a **re-scale month after two months of cuts** — the inverse of June/July’s “less spend, hold volume.” Spend came back, platform EAS came with it, but **true-attribution volume and acquisition did not**. All-channel, MoM (All WoW & MoM):

- Total spend **~$542K**, up **7%** MoM (still **30%** below May’s $769K peak)
- Platform EAS **1,816**, up **10%**
- EAS Actuals **1,286**, down **3%**
- AA Actuals **828**, down **13%**
- Cost/EAS Platform **$299**, down **3%** (best since February on this metric)
- Cost/EAS Actuals **$422**, up **11%**
- Cost/AA **$655**, up **22%** (vs. July’s matured **$535**, which remains 2026’s best)

The headline tension: **platform says August scaled efficiently; actuals say we bought more reported EAS and fewer acquisitions.** Spend +7% / Platform EAS +10% is a clean platform scaling story. On UTM actuals, EAS slipped and AA fell faster than spend, so Cost/AA moved the wrong way. Part of the AA gap is maturation (July itself revised ~+4% AA between snapshots; May had revised ~+11%). Even a generous +10% August AA revision (~910) would still leave Cost/AA well above July.

Channel takeaways:

- **Meta absorbed most of the re-scale and held actuals efficiency.** Spend +7% ($276K → $295K), Platform EAS +10% (950 → 1,047), EAS Actuals +8% (356 → 384). Cost/EAS Actuals **held flat at $769** (vs. July’s $775) — the July actuals dip did **not** deepen. AA still fell 191 → 173 (−9%). Platform Cost/EAS **$282**, a post-blackout best besides June’s $272.
- **Google’s efficiency recovery stalled as spend came back.** Spend +8% ($198K → $214K) produced only +1% EAS Actuals (566 → 572) and −2% AA (405 → 397). Cost/EAS Actuals **$350 → $374**. July had matched April ($350–$353); August gave ~7% of that back. Google still delivered **48% of AA** on **39% of spend**.
- **Bing was scaled again and broke the same way as May.** Spend **$3.0K → $11.0K** (+262%) for the same **8 EAS**; Cost/EAS **$381 → $1,380**. AA 9 → 13 does not justify a 3.6× budget.
- **TikTok platform and actuals split violently.** Spend cut **26%** ($30K → $22K); Platform EAS **+53%** (77 → 118) and Cost/EAS Platform **$383 → $186** (best month since launch). EAS Actuals **14 → 7** and AA **9 → 3** — July’s AA jump did **not** confirm. Weeks of 8/16 and 8/23 show 34 and 49 Platform EAS against 0 and 1 actuals.
- **Audio + CTV** remain off. **Unknown** is still **~24% of AA** (198 of 828), similar to the ~25% gap flagged since March.
- **Early September is a spend pullback.** First two days average **~$12.8K/day** vs. August’s **~$17.5K** (~$384K monthly run-rate, −29% vs. August). Too early to read actuals.

## Google, Meta & Bing Combined Metrics

Paid group as defined on the tab (Meta + Search + Bing + Other + Unknown; excludes TikTok/Audio/CTV). MoM:

- Combined spend **$520K**, up **9%**
- Platform EAS **1,698**, up **8%**
- EAS Actuals **1,279**, down **3%**
- AA Actuals **825**, down **12%**
- Cost/EAS Platform **$306**, roughly **flat** (+1%)
- Cost/EAS Actuals **$407**, up **12%**
- Cost/AA **$631**, up **24%** (July’s $508 was the group’s best of 2026)

Key points:

- **This is the first month since May that combined spend rose**, and the actuals response looks like a milder version of May: more dollars, slightly fewer attributed EAS, materially fewer AA. June/July’s “cut spend, hold (or improve) unit economics” pattern reversed.
- **Platform and actuals diverged again after three months of tighter alignment.** Paid Cost/EAS Platform stayed ~$304–$306 Jul→Aug while Actuals moved $362 → $407. Reported platform is no longer a clean proxy this month — the gap is Meta- and Unknown-driven; Google is still tight.
- **Within-month, mid-August was the weak window; the close improved on actuals.** Paid weekly Cost/EAS Actuals: 8/02 **$411** → 8/09 **$378** → 8/16 **$477** (worst) → 8/23 **$400**. The 8/16 week coincides with Meta’s worst actuals week ($949) and Google’s $420. Calendar week of 8/24–8/31 (from Aug Summary daily; not yet a full row on Paid WoW) ran ~$380 Cost/EAS Actuals on lower daily spend — a better exit than mid-month, not as strong as the 7/26 week.
- **AA mix is still Google-anchored.** Google 397 AA (48%), Meta 173 (21%), Unknown 198 (24%), Other 44, Bing 13, TikTok 3. Re-scaling Meta/Google spend did not convert Unknown or Meta into a larger share of acquisitions.

## Google Metrics

Third month of the Phase 2/3 recovery was interrupted by an **8% spend add-back**. MoM:

- Spend **$214K**, up **8%** (still **37%** below May’s $340K)
- Platform EAS **643**, up **5%**
- EAS Actuals **572**, up **1%**
- AA **397**, down **2%**
- Cost/EAS Platform **$333**, up **3%**
- Cost/EAS Actuals **$374**, up **7%**

Key points:

- **Re-scaling reintroduced diminishing returns, at a smaller scale than May.** +8% spend bought +1% EAS Actuals. That is the same shape as May (+24% spend / −5% Platform EAS) without the blowout magnitude. Late-July weeks at ~$31–$34K spend ran **$250–$323** Cost/EAS Actuals; August weeks at **$51–$55K** ran **$353–$420**. Efficiency at the throttled operating point did not survive the return toward $50K+/week.
- **The three-month recovery arc is no longer monotonic.** Cost/EAS Actuals: **$395 (May) → $368 (June) → $350 (July) → $374 (August)**. July had closed the gap to April ($352). August sits between June and July — not a collapse, but not “on track to February $303” either.
- **Platform–actuals alignment held.** $333 vs $374 (a $41 gap; July was $324 vs $350). Google’s reported numbers can still be read near face value.
- **Google remains the acquisition engine.** 48% of AA at **$539** Cost/AA Actuals — still the efficient lower-funnel channel. Volume is high-intent; the issue is elasticity as spend rises, not channel quality.
- **Week of 8/23 already showed the tax of lower spend again:** $44K / 117 Platform EAS / **$413** Cost/EAS Actuals — spend down vs. 8/16 but unit cost did not improve. Re-scale decisions should use the $50K-week band (~$370–$420), not the 7/26 $250 print, as the planning assumption.

## Meta Metrics

August **stabilized the July actuals reversal** and took incremental spend without another efficiency break. MoM:

- Spend **$295K**, up **7%**
- Platform EAS **1,047**, up **10%**
- EAS Actuals **384**, up **8%**
- AA **173**, down **9%**
- Cost/EAS Platform **$282**, down **3%** (2nd-best month since February, after June $272)
- Cost/EAS Actuals **$769**, roughly **flat** (−1% vs. July’s matured $775)

Key points:

- **On actuals, August looks like a volume recovery at July’s unit cost — not a return to June.** June remains the outlier (EAS Actuals 524, Cost/EAS Actuals **$609**). July 356 EAS at $775 → August 384 at $769. Spend and EAS Actuals moved together (~+7–8%), which is the healthy relationship that broke in July (then: −14% spend / −33% actuals in the July-deck snapshot). The UTM-changeover diagnosis from July is consistent with this: once the new UTM stabilized, actuals scaled with spend again.
- **Platform still flatters the channel ~2.7×** (1,047 vs 384), unchanged from July’s 2.67×. Do not put $282 next to Google’s $333 as an apples-to-apples efficiency ranking; on actuals Meta is ~2.1× Google ($769 vs $374).
- **Weekly pattern rhymes with July: mid-month actuals dip, month-end recovery.** Cost/EAS Actuals: 8/02 **$850** → 8/09 **$720** (best week; 280 Platform EAS at $231 Platform) → 8/16 **$949** (worst, same role as 7/12) → 8/23 **$653**. The 8/23 week ($653 actuals / $274 platform) is in range of the 7/26 recovery week ($675 / $250). Whatever hits mid-month is repeating, then partially correcting — worth a campaign-level check on Consolidated Test vs Evergreen again, not a new structural narrative.
- **AA is the weak Meta number.** EAS Actuals up, AA down. If it holds after maturation, August Meta traffic converted to acquisition less well than July — opposite of the account-level July story (fewer EAS, better AA). Flag, don’t bury; sample is still small week to week (33–43 AA).
- **September pacing on Meta is down.** Tab run-rate ~$236K (−20% vs August) at ~$262 Platform Cost/EAS on a two-day sample.

Suggested diagnostic line (July follow-up): August EAS actuals moving in line with spend supports treating July as a **measurement/transition month on Consolidated Test**, not a lasting unwind of the 5/4–5/5 structural work. June $609 actuals is still the proof point; July–August $769–$775 is the current stable range. Closing that gap is the Meta job for Q3, not celebrating $282 platform.

## Bing Metrics

Bing was taken off maintenance and **re-tested at May-like spend — and failed the same test**. MoM:

- Spend **$11.0K**, up **262%** (May peak was $12.5K)
- EAS **8**, **flat** (same as June and July)
- AA **13**, up from 9
- Cost/EAS **$1,380**, vs. $381 in July and **$1,080** at the May peak

Key points:

- **The “efficient only when small” learning repeated.** June/July: 8 EAS on $4.2K then $3.0K. August: 8 EAS on $11K. Incremental ~$8K bought **zero** incremental EAS. Cost/EAS is worse than the failed May scale-up.
- **Weekly spend held ~$2.4K after a hot 8/02 week ($3.1K, $1,569 Cost/EAS).** 8/09 was the only efficient-looking week ($198 Platform / $595 Actuals Cost/EAS, 12 Platform EAS, 4 actuals, 6 AA) — and it did not persist. 8/16 and 8/23 returned to ~$2.4K spend at $2.4K and $2.4K Cost/EAS Actuals on 1 actual EAS each.
- **AA 13 is not a scaling argument** at $849 Cost/AA. At this size one or two conversions move the rate.
- **Platform tracking is still unreliable at this scale** (All WoW shows 33 Platform EAS vs. the Bing tab’s 8). UTM actuals remain the read. Until Cost/EAS holds under ~$400 as weekly spend stays above $2K — the bar suggested in July — this is a **failed re-test**, not a new volume pocket.

## TikTok Metrics & Performance

August is the **mirror image of July**: platform looks like a breakthrough; true attribution went the other way. MoM:

- Spend **$22.0K**, down **26%**
- Platform EAS **118**, up **53%**
- EAS Actuals **7**, down **50%** (14 → 7)
- AA **3**, back to the 2–3/month band (July had 9)
- Cost/EAS Platform **$186**, down **51%** (best since launch)
- Implied Cost/AA **~$7.3K**, back near June

Key points:

- **July’s 9 AA did not repeat.** The right slide line is the one written in July: “needs August confirmation.” Confirmation failed. Treat July as a one-month spike, not an upper-funnel inflection.
- **Do not report $186 Cost/EAS as channel performance without the actuals caveat.** Weeks of 8/16 ($117 Platform Cost/EAS, **0** actuals) and 8/23 ($80 Platform, **1** actual) are almost certainly platform over-reporting, not a 2× efficiency gain. Blended actuals Cost/EAS **$3,137**.
- **Spend share stayed test-scale** (~4% of account). Cutting 26% while platform EAS rose is only a good story if those EAS are real; they are not showing up in UTM.
- Weekly actuals remain unusable for decisions (0–2 EAS). Monthly grain only.

## August Key Takeaways

- **Re-scaling spent the efficiency July earned.** Combined paid +9% spend produced −3% EAS Actuals and −12% AA. Platform EAS (+8–10%) and Platform Cost/EAS (flat to slightly better) will overstate the month if they lead the deck. Lead with actuals, footnote maturation.
- **Meta is stable, not back to June.** July’s actuals scare looks measurement-driven: August EAS Actuals tracked spend and Cost/EAS Actuals held ~$769. The 5/4–5/5 structure is not broken — it also is not delivering June’s $609. Mid-month actuals dips (7/12, 8/16) are a repeating pattern to inspect at campaign level.
- **Google is not ready for May-like spend.** The throttle worked (May $395 → July $350 Cost/EAS Actuals). Putting ~$50K+ weeks back on the channel moved it to **$374** and a +8% spend / +1% EAS Actuals elasticity. Hold or phase spend up only if weekly Cost/EAS Actuals stays in the high-$300s, not on the 7/26 $250 print.
- **Bing re-scale should be unwound.** Same result as May: 3–4× spend, flat EAS, four-digit Cost/EAS. Return to the $3K maintenance pocket.
- **TikTok’s July AA story is off the table until it repeats.** Platform $186 is not decision-grade. Keep test-scale; do not use platform EAS to argue for budget.
- **Unknown is still ~1 in 4 AAs.** Channel-level ROI remains incomplete. August AA 828 vs. May 993 with 30% less spend is still a better acquisition-per-dollar picture than May ($655 vs $775 Cost/AA) — but July ($535) is the efficiency benchmark, and August moved away from it.
